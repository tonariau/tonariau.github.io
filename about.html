Hey! My name is Nariel Arthur and I'm a multimedia designer and artist from São Paulo, Brazil. My skills include graphic design, user interface, and illustration. I constantly seek to integrate experimentation and theoretical foundations to my creative work, which is full of textures, abstractionism, pixel art, and asian pop aesthetics.

I have 8+ years of experience and have worked for agencies, consultacies, companies and institutions, contributing for campaigns and strategies regarding marketing, events, social media, digital products, and branding. In addition, I earned a scholarship at Apple Developer Academy in 2020 and was elected an Ambassador the following year, where I had the opportunity to develop authorial iOS applications and participate in exclusive conferences and workshops.

I currently work as a freelancer while dedicating to personal and academic projects; in my spare time, I'm an internet heavy user and a fan of retro games, which are huge references in my creations too.
